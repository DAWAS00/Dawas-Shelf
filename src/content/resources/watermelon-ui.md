## Move from an example to a working interface

[Watermelon UI](https://ui.watermelon.sh/home) brings React components, animated interactions, reusable page sections, and dashboard templates into one catalog. Browse the visual examples, then follow the item's source and setup instructions.

## Try one component in context

Choose a small interaction your page needs, such as a tab switcher or expandable card. Ask your AI assistant to adapt it to your content, typography, and colors. Its [installation guide](https://ui.watermelon.sh/installation) explains the shadcn CLI setup, while framework support is documented separately.

## Check more than the animation

Test keyboard interaction, narrow screens, and reduced motion after adapting an example. Review its dependencies and license before reuse, and keep the surrounding interface consistent with your own design system.
