## Make the data easier to read

[Bklit UI](https://bklit.com/) offers chart components, examples, and recipes, with options including line, area, bar, pie, and more specialized visualizations. Its [installation guide](https://bklit.com/docs/installation) explains the shadcn/ui prerequisite and the `@bklit` registry.

## Start with the question

Decide what your reader needs to understand before choosing a chart. Give your AI assistant a small sample dataset and ask it to adapt one documented example to your dashboard, with clear labels and units.

## Review the real states

Check empty datasets, missing values, narrow screens, and keyboard access to important information. Review the component's dependencies and source before integrating it. The documentation also links to theming guidance and AI skills.
