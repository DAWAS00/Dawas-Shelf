## Follow an interesting thread

Hugging Face hosts machine learning models, datasets, and Spaces. Start with a model card or a demo related to something you are trying to understand.

## Look beyond the demo

Read the model card for intended uses, limitations, licensing, and evaluation details. Those details matter as much as an appealing example output.

## Explore deliberately

Community projects vary in scope and maintenance. Check the individual project’s documentation and terms before building on it or sharing data with it.
