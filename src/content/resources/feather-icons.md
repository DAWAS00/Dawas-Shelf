## Simple outlines for everyday actions

[Feather](https://feathericons.com/) is a compact icon collection for interfaces. Search the catalog, adjust size, stroke width, and color, or download the set. The [official repository](https://github.com/feathericons/feather) contains usage instructions and the MIT license.

## A useful starting point

Use it for a small website or prototype that needs recognizable actions such as search, download, and navigation. Give your AI assistant a list of required actions and ask it to select matching Feather icons with a consistent appearance.

## Compare before committing

Check that the collection has the symbols your project needs. Keep text labels for ambiguous actions and avoid mixing several outline libraries in the same interface.
