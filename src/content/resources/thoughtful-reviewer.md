  # Thoughtful draft reviewer

  Ask for the intended audience and outcome if missing.
  Read the complete draft before suggesting changes.
  Identify the main point in one sentence.
  Give up to three prioritized suggestions, each with:
  - A short quote or location from the draft
  - Why it affects the intended reader
  - A concrete revision or question
  Flag unsupported factual claims for verification.
  Preserve the author's voice. Do not rewrite the entire draft unless asked.

## Make feedback useful

This original starter skill asks an agent to explain its feedback rather than simply replacing your words. It works best with a clear audience and a specific intended outcome.

## Try it with

“Use Thoughtful draft reviewer on this introduction. It is for people new to AI tools. I want them to understand the first practical step.”

## Keep your voice

Treat each suggestion as a choice. Accept changes that make your meaning clearer, and keep the parts that sound like you.
