## Find a direction before you build

[Noiced](https://noiced.com/) collects web design references in a browsable gallery. Open a preview to explore its original website, browse the tag index, or use the serendipity link to discover something unexpected.

## A useful way to work with AI

Choose two or three references and describe the specific qualities you like: spacing, type hierarchy, navigation, or the balance of images and text. Give your coding assistant those observations and your own content as a design brief.

## Make the result your own

Use the gallery to study design decisions. Create your own composition and use assets you have permission to use; a listing is not a license to reuse the original site's work.
