  I would like a thoughtful second perspective on this idea:

  [Describe your idea, decision, or plan]

  Context: [What matters, and what are the constraints?]

  Please:
  1. Restate my strongest argument fairly.
  2. Identify the three assumptions that matter most.
  3. Suggest a plausible alternative interpretation.
  4. Explain what evidence would change your assessment.
  5. Propose one small, reversible experiment I can try.

  Distinguish facts from assumptions. Ask about missing context.
  Be direct and constructive, without inventing certainty.

## Create room for another angle

Use this prompt when you have a plan and want to see its weak spots before investing more time. Give the assistant the constraints that really matter.

## Take it into the world

Choose the smallest experiment from the response. Real feedback from the people affected by your idea is more useful than agreement from an assistant.
