  [Paste your idea here]

  Audience: [Who is this for?]
  Desired outcome: [What should change for the audience?]
  Format: [Article, video, website, or something else]
  Constraints: [Time, length, tone, and things to avoid]

  First, ask up to three questions if essential information is missing.
  Then write a concise brief with:
  - The single main idea
  - The audience and their real need
  - The desired response
  - Three possible angles, with a reason for each
  - A practical outline and a clear next step

  Keep assumptions explicit. Use plain language and specific examples.

## Give the idea a little structure

Use this prompt when you know roughly what you want to make but are still deciding what it should say. Fill in the bracketed fields before sending it to your preferred assistant.

## Make it yours

Add one example of work you like and explain what makes it effective. Ask the assistant to use those qualities without copying the original.

## A useful next step

Choose one angle and write the first paragraph yourself. Use the brief to keep the work focused, while leaving room for your own judgment and voice.
