## Learn by reading a skill

This repository contains examples of Agent Skills. Open a small example and look at how its purpose, instructions, and supporting files fit together.

## Before installing anything

Read the individual skill, check its license and requirements, and follow the repository’s current setup guidance. Some examples may require tools or services you have not enabled.

## A useful exercise

Compare an example with [Research companion](/resources/research-companion/). Notice what a skill needs to explain for an agent to apply it consistently.
