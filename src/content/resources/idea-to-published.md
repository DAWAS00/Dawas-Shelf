## Start with something real

Choose a question someone actually asked, a problem you solved, or an observation from your own work. Save any source links alongside your notes.

## Make the point smaller

Use [The clearer creative brief](/resources/creative-brief/) to identify one main idea. A useful post answers a focused question; it does not need to cover a whole field.

## Add the human part

Include your own example and what you learned from it. Read the draft aloud, remove generic advice, and verify factual statements against their original sources.
