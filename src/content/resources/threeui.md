## Explore motion and depth

[ThreeUI](https://threeui.com/browse) brings together Three.js scenes, shader effects, backgrounds, buttons, text animation, and complete page templates. Browse by category and open an item to examine its preview and available source.

## Start with one purposeful effect

Choose a component that supports your page's message, such as a hero scene or a subtle background. Ask your AI assistant to adapt its layout and colors to your project, then inspect the dependencies and integration instructions before adding it.

## Check the experience on real devices

Test rendering cost, touch interaction, and a reduced-motion fallback. The catalog includes Free and Pro entries; check the selected item's access and license before reuse.
