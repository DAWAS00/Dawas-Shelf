## Define the question

Give yourself a scope and a stopping point. “How do these two approaches differ?” is easier to investigate than “Tell me everything about this topic.”

## Keep evidence beside the claim

Use [Research companion](/resources/research-companion/) to organize the search. Keep source links and dates in the same notes as the claims they support.

## Finish with your understanding

Write three things you learned, one point of disagreement, and one question still worth investigating. This makes your notes a starting point for your next session.
