## Explore reusable knowledge for your agent

[skills.sh](https://skills.sh/) is an agent-skills directory from Vercel. Search the listings, explore topics and packs, or compare the all-time, trending, and hot views. Entries identify the skill and its source so you can investigate it further.

## Find a skill that fits your workflow

Choose a task, open a candidate, and read the skill's instructions and source repository. Check support for the agent you use. The site's [documentation](https://skills.sh/docs) is the starting point for its CLI and installation workflow.

## Treat popularity as a discovery signal

Review authorship, permissions, scripts, and dependencies before installing anything. Try a new skill on a small task and inspect the result. Installing a skill gives an agent additional instructions; it does not guarantee a correct outcome.
