## Give the design a written starting point

[designmd.ai](https://designmd.ai/) is a community library of design systems shared as DESIGN.md files. Browse featured, trending, and newly added entries, or search tags for a visual direction that fits your project.

## Bring the specification into your project

Choose a system, read the file, and adapt it to your own product. Add it beside your project documentation and explicitly ask your AI coding tool to follow it when building or revising screens. The site also links to MCP and CLI options.

## Make the rules concrete

Use a specification to agree on colors, typography, spacing, and component behavior. Check the individual entry's authorship and reuse terms, and review the generated interface against the file rather than assuming the assistant followed every rule.
