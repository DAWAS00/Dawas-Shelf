## Find the right weight for your interface

[Phosphor](https://phosphoricons.com/) offers a searchable icon collection with different visual weights, color and size controls, and resources for design and development. Its [official core package](https://github.com/phosphor-icons/core) provides the SVG assets grouped by weight.

## How this shelf uses it

Dawas Shlef uses Phosphor for Instagram, navigation arrows, search, bookmarks, and copy controls. A shared component keeps those icons visually consistent without loading an external icon font.

## Try it with your AI assistant

Choose one weight for ordinary actions and a filled variant for selected states. Ask the assistant to keep accessible button labels and to import the specific icons your project needs.
