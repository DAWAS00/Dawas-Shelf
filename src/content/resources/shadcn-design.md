## Give your assistant a visual brief

[shadcn.io Design](https://www.shadcn.io/design) provides curated DESIGN.md specifications describing colors, typography, and components drawn from real brands. The page presents these design files as free resources for working with coding assistants such as Claude or Cursor.

## Turn a reference into your own system

Choose a specification, read its design decisions, and adapt the file to your own palette, fonts, spacing, and component rules. Include it in your project's context so an assistant has concrete instructions to follow while building screens.

## Keep your identity clear

Use brand examples as references for learning. Create your own identity and check any applicable asset or font licenses. Other offerings on shadcn.io have separate access and licensing terms.
